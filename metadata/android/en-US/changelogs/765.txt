## Changes

This release restores and enhances features and sensor support from the 3.x app, while adding major capabilities including:

• In-app firmware updates
• OLED display instrument and retro games
• Multimeter continuity-beep mode
• Oscilloscope stacked and buffer-overlay modes
• Sound classification in the sound meter
• Labrador board support
* Builds for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux, and the web
